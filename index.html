Research

My research is in applied statistics and quantitative social science. With Professor Sam Zhang, I'm working to uncover patterns in faculty hiring and attrition. More broadly, I'm interested in how data science and statistical modeling can drive meaningful social impact.

That work happens in the {{ site.lab_name }} at the Vermont Complex Systems Institute, a group of mathematicians and data scientists modeling the human side of science.

Before the PhD I spent three years as a data scientist at Noblis, analyzing the face image quality and facial recognition systems behind U.S. passport and visa applications. I hold an MS in Statistics from the University of Vermont and a BS in Mathematics from the University of California, Irvine.

Methods
Machine learning, deep learning, statistical network analysis, natural language processing, computer vision
Tools
Python (scikit-learn, TensorFlow, pandas, NumPy), R, SAS, Tableau, Git
